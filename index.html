About me

Saxion CMGT game design student in Enschede, focused on systems and mechanics design, balancing and level design. I author Game Design Documents, prototype them in Unity with C# and Photon networking, and refine them through playtesting. Alongside design I model and texture in Maya and Substance Painter, and design interfaces in Adobe XD. Completed a game design course under Gavin Yeo, Design Director at EA / Criterion Games.
